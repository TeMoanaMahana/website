Henk van Rossem one of the seagoing technicians, he is a Principal Technician with Earth Sciences New Zealand and supports glider deployments and recoveries. He grew up and studied engineering in the Netherlands before moving to New Zealand in the early 2000s. At ESNZ he is the operations lead for the second largest Tsunami Warning Buoy Network in the world, covering New Zealand and the South Pacific.
